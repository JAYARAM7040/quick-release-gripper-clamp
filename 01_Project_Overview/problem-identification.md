
# Problem Identification

## Existing Design

The existing gripper clamp uses a bolt and nut arrangement to hold the component securely during the machining operation.

## Identified Problem

- Complete removal of the bolt and nut is required to remove the component.
- The removal and refitting process takes additional time.
- Repeated bolt handling increases the tool-changing effort.
- The existing arrangement makes quick component removal difficult.

## Need for Modification

A design modification is required to simplify component removal while maintaining proper clamping and bolt engagement.

## Proposed Improvement

A U-slot arrangement is introduced in the gripper clamp to allow component removal by slightly loosening the bolt instead of completely removing it.
